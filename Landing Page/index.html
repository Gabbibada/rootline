What Rootline does

Built around the connections that matter

Relationship engine

Our graph algorithm finds the shortest path between any two people and generates a natural-language label — "Your first cousin once removed" — automatically, every time.

Claim your profile

Family members get an invite link to claim their own node, add photos, and keep their own info current. Everyone owns their own branch.

Birthday reminders

Never forget a birthday again. Rootline surfaces upcoming birthdays across the whole tree and tells you who they are and how they're related to you.

Live sync

Realtime updates keep every family member's app in sync. When someone adds a new branch, everyone sees it the moment it's saved.

Visual tree

A clean, scrollable family tree makes the whole picture visible at once. Tap any node to see the full profile and their connection to you.

Private by default

Row-level security on every table. Your family data stays yours — no scraping, no profiling, no surprises.
