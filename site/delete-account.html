Rootline — account & data deletion

You can ask us to permanently delete your Rootline account and the personal data associated with it at any time. This page explains how to make the request and exactly what is removed.

How to request deletion

  1. From the email address associated with your Rootline account, email us at the address below.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my account".
  3. We will verify the request, permanently delete your account and data, and email you to confirm — normally within 30 days.
rootline@gabbibada.com

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your login credentials (email address and password).
  • Your profile information (name, nickname, date of birth, birthplace, occupation, location, gender, and story).
  • The family members and tree data you created.
  • Photos you uploaded.
  • Your notification preferences.

What we keep

We do not retain your personal data after deletion, except where we are legally required to keep limited records. Any such data is removed once the legal obligation ends. We never keep your data for marketing — Rootline has no ads and does not sell personal information.

Delete data without closing your account

If you would rather keep your account, you can delete individual people, photos, and details directly in the app at any time. To remove everything, use the email request above.

Questions? Email rootline@gabbibada.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
